Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase Analytics (now officially called Google Analytics for Firebase) is a free analytics platform from Google that helps you understand how users interact with your mobile app or website. Instead of just counting downloads, it tells you what users actually do after opening your app.

Think of it like a security camera for your app—not recording video, but recording user actions.

Simple Example

Imagine you have a shopping app.

Firebase Analytics can answer questions like:

  • How many users opened the app today?
  • Which screen is visited the most?
  • Which button is clicked the most?
  • How many users added products to the cart?
  • How many completed a purchase?
  • Which country has the most users?
  • Which marketing campaign brought the most users?
  • Where do users quit using the app?

Without analytics:


10,000 downloads

That's all you know.

With Firebase Analytics:


10,000 installs

8,000 users opened app
6,500 viewed products
4,100 added to cart
2,900 purchased

Purchase rate = 29%

Now you understand user behavior.

Benefits of Firebase Analytics

  • Free for most app analytics needs.
  • Easy SDK integration for Android, iOS, web, Flutter, and React Native.
  • Automatic event collection reduces development effort.
  • Custom events provide detailed business insights.
  • Real-time and historical reporting.
  • Audience segmentation for targeted analysis.
  • Integrates with other Firebase services like Crashlytics, Remote Config, A/B Testing, Cloud Messaging, and BigQuery for advanced analysis.

In summary

Firebase Analytics is a platform for collecting, organizing, and analyzing user behavior. It tells you who your users are, what they do, where they stop, and how they engage with your app. Combined with machine learning tools such as TensorFlow, the collected data can also be used to build predictive features like churn prediction, personalized recommendations, and intelligent user segmentation.
