Modern AI can help businesses

Modern AI can help businesses much more broadly than just “adding a chatbot.” The biggest opportunity is to use AI as a decision-making, automation, prediction, and optimization layer on top of existing business systems.

1. AI agents — automate business workflows

Instead of employees manually doing repetitive processes, an AI agent can:

  • Read incoming emails
  • Understand customer requests
  • Look up information in CRM/database
  • Decide what needs to happen
  • Call APIs
  • Create/update records
  • Send responses
  • Escalate unusual cases to humans

Example:

A sales inquiry arrives → AI understands the request → checks inventory → generates quotation → creates CRM lead → notifies salesperson → follows up automatically.

2. Predictive AI — predict what will happen

This is where non-LLM AI models become particularly valuable.

Businesses can use:

  • Time-series models → sales/demand forecasting
  • Classification models → customer churn, fraud, lead qualification
  • Regression models → pricing/revenue prediction
  • Anomaly detection → unusual transactions or equipment behavior
  • Recommendation models → products/content to recommend
  • Computer vision → quality inspection, counting, monitoring

For example:


Historical sales
       ↓
ML forecasting model
       ↓
Expected demand
       ↓
Inventory optimization
       ↓
Automatic purchasing recommendation

3. AI-powered CRM

For a platform like TryCastanet, AI could become significantly more powerful than a traditional contact manager.

Instead of simply storing:


John Smith
CEO
ABC Corp
Last contacted: 45 days ago

AI can produce:


John Smith
CEO — ABC Corp

Relationship health: 72/100

Why:
• No interaction in 43 days
• Previously contacted every ~30 days
• Last conversation mentioned expansion
• ABC Corp recently hired 12 employees

Recommended action:
→ Contact John this week

Suggested message:
→ Congratulate him on the company's expansion
   and ask about the new hiring initiative.

That's relationship intelligence, rather than just contact storage.

8. AI forecasting

Companies can ask:

“How many units will we need next month?”

AI can consider:

  • Historical sales
  • Seasonality
  • Holidays
  • Promotions
  • Weather
  • Pricing
  • Regional differences
  • Marketing campaigns

Then produce:


Product A
Next month forecast: 14,820 units
Expected range: 13,900–15,700

Recommended inventory: 15,400

The key idea

LLMs are only one component.

A modern enterprise AI system might combine:

AI capability Business use
LLM Reasoning, language, analysis
AI agents Workflow automation
RAG Company knowledge
Predictive ML Forecasting
Classification Risk/lead/churn scoring
Recommendation models Next-best action
Computer vision Visual inspection
Anomaly detection Fraud/monitoring
Optimization Scheduling/routes/pricing
Speech AI Calls/meetings
OCR/document AI Forms/invoices/contracts

If you're thinking about this from a product/business perspective, a particularly strong opportunity for you as a full-stack + AI developer is building vertical AI systems: take one industry (sales, real estate, logistics, healthcare, accounting, construction, etc.) and build an AI system that actually makes decisions and performs workflows, rather than simply chatting.
