Scrum Agile

Scrum Agile is a way of managing and completing projects—especially software projects—using the principles of Agile.

🔹 What is Agile?

Agile is a mindset or philosophy for building products:

  • Focuses on flexibility and adaptability
  • Encourages frequent updates and improvements
  • Values collaboration with customers
  • Delivers work in small, usable pieces

🔹 What is Scrum?

Scrum is one of the most popular frameworks used to apply Agile in practice.

It breaks work into short cycles called Sprints (usually 1–4 weeks), where a team plans, builds, and reviews a small part of the project.

🔹 Key Scrum Concepts

1. Roles

  • Product Owner → decides what should be built
  • Scrum Master → helps the team follow Scrum and removes obstacles
  • Development Team → builds the product

2. Events

  • Sprint Planning → decide what to work on
  • Daily Scrum (stand-up) → short daily progress meeting
  • Sprint Review → show completed work
  • Sprint Retrospective → discuss improvements

3. Artifacts

  • Product Backlog → list of all work needed
  • Sprint Backlog → tasks for the current sprint
  • Increment → finished product piece

🔹 Simple Example

Imagine building a mobile app:

  • Instead of making the whole app at once, Scrum teams:
    • Build login feature in Sprint 1
    • Add profile page in Sprint 2
    • Add messaging in Sprint 3

🔹 Why use Scrum Agile?

  • Faster delivery of features
  • Easier to handle changes
  • Better teamwork
  • Continuous improvement

In short:

Agile = mindset

Scrum = structured way to apply that mindset
