Area in mathematics

Area in mathematics means how much flat space a shape covers.

Think of it like this:

Area = the amount of surface inside a shape.

We usually measure area in square units, such as:

  • cm²
  • m²
  • km²
  • ft²

1. Rectangle

The easiest formula is:

Area = Length × Width

Example:

A room is 5 meters long and 4 meters wide.

A=5×4=20m2

So the room has 20 square meters of floor area.

2. Square

A square has equal sides.

Area = Side × Side

Example: A square has a side of 6 cm.

A=6×6=36cm2

3. Triangle

Area = ½ × Base × Height

Example:

Base = 10 m

Height = 6 m

Real-world applications

Area is extremely useful in everyday life.

🏠 Construction

If you want to buy flooring for a room:

Room = 6 m × 5 m


6×5=30m^2

You need enough flooring to cover approximately 30 m².

🎨 Painting

If a wall is 4 m wide and 3 m high:


4×3=12m^2

You need paint to cover 12 square meters.

🌾 Farming

If a rectangular farm is 100 m × 50 m:


100×50=5,000m^2

You can use this to determine how much land is available for planting.

💻 Programming / UI

Area can even appear in software development. For example, calculating the size of an image:


width = 800 pixels
height = 600 pixels

area = 800 × 600
     = 480,000 pixels²

This helps with things like image processing, computer graphics, game development, and computer vision.

The key idea

Area tells you "how much space is inside a 2D shape."

If you can identify the shape and know its dimensions, you can choose the appropriate formula and calculate the area.
