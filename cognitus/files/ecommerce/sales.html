Sales

Sales is the process of selling a product or service to customers in exchange for money. It involves understanding customer needs, presenting solutions, answering questions, negotiating, and completing the purchase.

For example:

  • A clothing store employee helping you choose and buy a jacket is doing sales.
  • A software company representative convincing a business to buy their software is also doing sales.

The main goals of sales are to:

  • Find potential customers.
  • Understand their needs.
  • Explain how a product or service can help.
  • Close the deal (make the sale).
  • Build long-term customer relationships.

In simple terms, sales is the activity of persuading customers to buy a product or service.

Sales Platform

A sales platform is software that helps businesses manage, automate, and improve the entire sales process—from finding potential customers to closing deals and maintaining customer relationships.

Think of it as a central workspace for a sales team.

What a sales platform typically does

  • Stores customer information (contacts, companies, communication history)
  • Tracks leads (people who might become customers)
  • Manages deals through different sales stages (e.g., New Lead → Contacted → Proposal → Won/Lost)
  • Automates repetitive tasks like follow-up emails and reminders
  • Provides reports and analytics to measure sales performance
  • Integrates with other tools such as email, calendars, marketing software, and accounting systems

Example

Imagine a company selling business software:

  1. A visitor fills out a form on the website.
  2. The sales platform automatically creates a new lead.
  3. A salesperson contacts the lead and records the conversation.
  4. Meetings, emails, and follow-ups are tracked.
  5. A proposal is sent.
  6. The deal is marked as Won once the customer purchases.

Everything is managed in one place instead of using spreadsheets and sticky notes.

Common sales platforms

Some well-known sales platforms include:

  • Salesforce – Enterprise CRM and sales management.
  • HubSpot – Popular CRM with a free tier.
  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 – CRM integrated with Microsoft products.
  • Pipedrive – Focused on visual sales pipelines.
  • Zoho – Affordable CRM and business suite.
  • Odoo – Includes CRM plus ERP, accounting, inventory, HR, and more.

Sales platform vs. CRM

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system focuses on storing and managing customer relationships.

A sales platform often includes CRM plus additional tools such as:

  • Sales pipeline management
  • Sales automation
  • Email tracking
  • Quote generation
  • Forecasting
  • Performance dashboards

Many modern CRMs, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, and Odoo, are also considered full sales platforms because they provide all of these capabilities.

In simple terms: A sales platform is software that helps businesses organize their sales activities, manage customers, and close more deals efficiently.
