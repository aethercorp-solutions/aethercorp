In-App Purchase (IAP)

In-App Purchase (IAP) is a way for users to buy digital content, features, or subscriptions inside a mobile app without leaving the app. The payment is processed by the platform (Apple App Store or Google Play Store), and the app unlocks the purchased content after successful payment.

Since you've been working with React Native and previously used react-native-iap, understanding how IAP works is very useful.

Simple Analogy

Imagine a video game.

You download the game for free.

Inside the game you can buy:

  • Extra lives
  • More coins
  • New characters
  • Premium membership

Those purchases are In-App Purchases.

The app itself doesn't collect your credit card.

Instead:

Common React Native IAP APIs

If you're using react-native-iap, you'll typically work with these methods:

Method Purpose
initConnection() Connect to the billing service when the app starts.
getProducts() Retrieve one-time purchase products.
getSubscriptions() Retrieve subscription products.
requestPurchase() Start a one-time purchase.
requestSubscription() Start a subscription purchase.
purchaseUpdatedListener() Listen for successful purchases.
purchaseErrorListener() Listen for purchase failures or cancellations.
finishTransaction() Tell the store the purchase has been processed so it isn't delivered repeatedly.
getAvailablePurchases() Restore owned non-consumables and active subscriptions.
endConnection() Close the billing connection when appropriate.

Common Errors

You've encountered some of these before.

E_IAP_NOT_AVAILABLE

Billing is unavailable.

Possible reasons:

  • Running on an unsupported simulator (especially for purchases)
  • App Store/Play Store not configured correctly
  • Billing service unavailable

skus is required

You called:


getProducts([])

instead of providing valid product IDs such as:


["premium_monthly"]

Previous request was cancelled

Usually caused by:

  • Starting another purchase before the previous one completed
  • Initializing the billing connection multiple times
  • Not properly finishing a previous transaction

Best Practices

  • Initialize the billing connection once when the app starts.
  • Fetch products or subscriptions before displaying your purchase screen.
  • Validate receipts on your backend, not just in the app.
  • Call finishTransaction() after successfully processing a purchase.
  • Provide a Restore Purchases option for users.
  • Listen for purchase updates, because purchases may complete after the app resumes.
  • Handle cancellations and network errors gracefully.

Understanding this complete lifecycle is essential because every production-quality IAP implementation revolves around the same core steps: display products → initiate purchase → receive transaction → validate with the store → grant entitlements → restore and renew when necessary. Once you understand that flow, implementing IAP in React Native, native iOS (StoreKit), or Android (Google Play Billing) becomes much easier.
