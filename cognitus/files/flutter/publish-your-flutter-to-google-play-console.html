Publish your Flutter app to Google Play Console for Internal Testing

If you mean how to publish your Flutter app to Google Play Console for Internal Testing, the flow is:

1. Build a signed release AAB

From your Flutter project:


flutter clean
flutter pub get

flutter build appbundle --release

Your AAB will normally be here:


build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab

Since you already have an upload-keystore.jks, make sure your Flutter Android release signing is configured correctly.

2. Increase the version

In pubspec.yaml:


version: 1.0.1+2

The important part for Google Play is the build number:


    1.0.1+2
       ↑
   versionCode

Every uploaded build needs a higher version code.

For example:


1.0.0+1
1.0.1+2
1.0.2+3

3. Open Google Play Console

Go to:

Google Play Console

Select your app.

Then go to:

Testing → Internal testing

4. Create an internal testing release

Click:

Create new release

Upload:


build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab

Then enter release notes, for example:


Initial internal testing release.

Click Save → Review release → Start rollout to Internal testing.

5. Add your testers

Inside Internal testing, open the Testers tab.

You can create an email list such as:


paul@example.com
tester1@example.com
tester2@example.com

Then save the tester list.

Google Play will provide a test invitation/opt-in link.

6. Tester installs the app

The tester:

  1. Opens the opt-in link.
  2. Signs into Google using the tester email.
  3. Joins the test.
  4. Opens Google Play.
  5. Installs the app.

The app is distributed through Google Play just like a normal Play Store app, but only to your internal testers.

Recommended Flutter CI/CD flow

For your project, I would structure it like:


GitHub
   │
   ├── feature/*
   │      │
   │      └── Developer testing
   │
   └── development
          │
          ▼
      GitHub Actions
          │
          ▼
    Flutter build AAB
          │
          ▼
   Google Play Internal
       Testing
          │
          ▼
       Testers

Then later:


development
     ↓
Google Play Internal Testing
     ↓
Closed Testing
     ↓
Production

For TestFlight, you can use the same development branch to build an iOS archive and upload it to App Store Connect.
